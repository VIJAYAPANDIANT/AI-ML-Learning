## 📌 Project Overview
Academic research intelligence platform that ingests arXiv and conference papers, indexes sections (Abstract, Methodology, Empirical Results, Limitations), and synthesizes multi-paper comparative literature reviews.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Algorithms
- **Ingestion:** ArXiv API, LaTeX & PDF Parsing
- **Retrieval:** Multi-Vector Retrieval, Parent Document Retriever
- **Synthesis:** Map-Reduce Summarization & Comparative Matrix Generation
